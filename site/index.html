10MinMine is a fixed-supply mining coin on Sui.

Bitcoin-style issuance, with a new block about every ten minutes. 10MM has a 21,000,000 hard cap, eight decimals, and rewards that halve on a long-term schedule.

When blocks are mined, rewards are shared with holders. Connect a wallet to check your balance, claim rewards, or trade 10MM through the configured router.

How rewards work

  1. Buy or receive 10MM into your wallet.
  2. Every ~10 minutes a mine call advances the schedule.
  3. Your share is based on how much you hold and hold age.
  4. Rewards are paid directly during mine; claim remains a fallback for dust or legacy balances.

Supply rules

Hard cap 21M. Genesis 50 is permanently locked. No extra minting beyond the block schedule. After the long emission era, new 10MM stops; trading and fee-based tips can continue.

Built for Sui

10MinMine uses Sui wallet standards and public network data. Check live balances and token activity with 10mmscan.
